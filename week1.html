Production & Posting Notes

Visual template

  • Dark background: Monday deep blue, Tuesday dark navy/teal, Wednesday dark purple/red, Thursday charcoal/sepia, Friday deep green/dark orange.
  • White bold sans-serif for questions. Yellow or green for correct answers.
  • Timer top-right, large enough to read on a phone.
  • No transitions, no zoom effects, no stock footage.

Audio

  • One ticking sound during the countdown — same tick every time.
  • One “ding” or soft chime on the reveal.
  • Optional AI voiceover. Keep it flat and fast. No hype voice.

Text timing rule

The question and all four options must be fully readable in under 3 seconds. If it takes longer, cut words.

Caption and hashtags

Use 4–6 hashtags max: one broad (#quiz), one niche (#geographyquiz / #sciencequiz), one identity (#trivia), one discovery (#fyp). Do not stuff 15 hashtags.

After posting

  • Pin a comment: “Be honest—what did you get? 👇”
  • Reply to the first 10–15 comments within the first hour.
  • Check analytics at 24 hours: completion rate, replays, shares, profile visits.

Analytics diagnosis

SignalProblemFix
Completion below 30%Hook is weak or Q1 is too hardMake Q1 easier; put the question on screen in under 2 seconds
Low replaysReveal isn’t satisfying enoughAdd a clearer “aha” explanation on the reveal
Low sharesQuestions aren’t surprising or identity-affirmingAdd more “sounds wrong but is true” or “only [group]” framing
High profile visits, low followsBio or pinned video isn’t convertingBio: “Geography, science, culture, history, and sports quizzes 5x a week.” Pin your best video.

First 30-video rule

Do not change the template. The algorithm needs repetition to classify you, and viewers need repetition to recognize you. After 30 posts, adjust the pillar ratio based on which day overperforms.
